⚠️ PROBLEM STATEMENT • SLIDE 03

Challenges in Traditional Civic Maintenance

Municipal corporations struggle with manual workflows that slow down hazard resolution:

⏳
Delayed Triage
Manual site inspection of potholes and water leaks takes days to estimate repair costs.
🚫
Duplicate Spam
Hundreds of citizens lodge separate tickets for the same pothole, clogging municipal queues.
🚒
Wasteful Dispatch
Maintenance trucks drive unoptimized criss-cross routes across the city, wasting fuel.
📄
Zero Transparency
Citizens cannot track resolution SLA deadlines or official work orders in real time.
